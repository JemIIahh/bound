## 4. Payee verification as a market

- UK CoP: [S] >2.5 billion CoP checks completed since 2020 launch. PSR-published Frontier Economics evaluation (2026-07-01): APP fraud losses through Faster Payments down ~21% (~GBP 73M a year), ~35,000 fewer APP scams; reimbursement policy effective 2024-10-07; reimbursement in 97% of covered claims; cap cut from GBP 415,000 to GBP 85,000. NOTE: this is the effect of the APP REIMBURSEMENT REGIME as a whole, NOT CoP alone. Do not attribute the 21% to CoP. https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/07/290056-uk-payment-systems-regulator-psr-confirms-app-fraud-reimbursement-policy-delivers-strong-positive-results/ ; PSR https://www.psr.org.uk/our-work/app-scams/ . A CoP-specific fraud-reduction figure: NOT FOUND/UNVERIFIED.
- EU VoP: [S] From 2025-10-09 eurozone PSPs must offer VoP (name + IBAN check) free of charge to payers, for instant and non-instant credit transfers; EPC VoP scheme rulebook in force 2025-10-05. Sources: https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/document-library/press-releases/verification-payee-scheme-rulebook-now-force ; https://legal.pwc.de/en/news/articles/verification-of-payee-requirements-vop-under-the-eus-instant-payments-regulation-ipr
- Nacha: [S] Fraud-monitoring rule Phase 1 effective 2026-03-20 (all ODFIs; originators/TPS/TPSPs with >6M entries in 2023); Phase 2 effective 2026-06-19 (all non-consumer originators, TPSPs, TPSs); RDFIs must have risk-based processes to identify credit entries suspected unauthorized or authorized under false pretenses. NOTE: it is a monitoring rule, not a payee-name-match mandate. https://www.nacha.org/rules/risk-management-topics-fraud-monitoring-phase-1 ; https://www.nacha.org/rules/risk-management-topics-fraud-monitoring-phase-2

## Contradictions / risks vs claims in the brief

- $226B claim: confirmed (annualized, Dec 2025, McKinsey x Artemis), but only via secondary summaries; and it's payments not "volume", with 0.01% penetration caveat.
- "iPiD raised $16M Sep 2026 for stablecoin payee verification": raise confirmed, but it is a bank-account-verification company whose stablecoin work is a planned expansion, not the stated main use of proceeds.
- Meigi is not a distant analog: nearly identical concept (DNS proof + legal registry name + agent vault + x402 guard) already built at ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026 on Ethereum Sepolia; no Tempo enforcement, no stars. Judges will find it.
- Tempo access-key recipient allowlists: real and documented, but only constrain TIP-20 transfer/approve selectors; TIP-1011 page labels itself proposed while T3 upgrade notes say activated on mainnet 2026-04-27; re-verify.
- Agent-payments data is small ($73M in a year) and mostly micropayments in USDC (98.6%), not supplier invoices; the "AI agents pay invoices" claim is a forecast, not a measured trend.
- Nigeria: strong for founder-market fit (1st in cross-border and P2P flows), but the Chainalysis data measures crypto flows, not supplier invoice payments; no sourced stat on Nigerian B2B supplier payments in stablecoins was confirmed (Yellow Card B2B pivot is anecdotal support).
