## Outreach templates (each under 90 words)

Segment A, template 1 (Tempo team)
Hi, I'm building Bound: an onchain registry where suppliers self-verify (domain plus legal name), and Tempo access-key recipient allowlists enforce it so an AI agent can't pay a lookalike wallet. It targets "we changed our wallet" invoice fraud and address poisoning. I'd like 15 minutes to show it live on Tempo testnet (mainnet next) and ask what the ecosystem team would need to feature it. Would you have time this week? Oluwaseun, founder of Bound

Segment A, template 2 (builder)
Hi, I'm working on payee verification for Tempo payments. Your product lets [wallets/agents] send stablecoins to suppliers; Bound checks the recipient against a supplier-verified registry before the payment goes out, and can write the result into an access-key allowlist. Could we do a 15-minute call? If it looks useful, I'd like a short letter of intent to run it as a pilot. Oluwaseun, founder of Bound

Segment B, template 1 (payment company)
Hi, businesses paying suppliers in stablecoins get hit by "our wallet changed" invoice fraud. Bound lets suppliers verify their wallet against their domain and legal name once, free, and lets you check any payee by API before you settle. I'm in Abuja and building on Tempo. Could I have 15 minutes to show it and hear how your clients handle payee changes today? Oluwaseun, founder of Bound

Segment B, template 2 (supplier recruitment via partner)
Hi, I'd like to invite a few of your Nigerian business clients who receive USDC/USDT from overseas buyers to verify their wallet on Bound. It's free for suppliers and takes a few minutes; buyers can then confirm the wallet is really theirs. Could we talk for 15 minutes about a pilot, and would you sign a short letter of intent to introduce five suppliers? Oluwaseun, founder of Bound

Segment C, template 1 (agent infra)
Hi, when an agent pays a supplier invoice, a tricked agent can send funds to a lookalike wallet. Bound offers verify_payee (API and MCP): it checks the wallet against a registry of self-verified suppliers, and Tempo allowlists enforce the result at chain level. I'd like 15 minutes to demo it in your SDK flow. Would a letter of intent for a pilot integration be possible? Oluwaseun, founder of Bound

Segment C, template 2 (AP automation)
Hi, as AP moves to stablecoins, the risk moves from bank-detail changes to wallet-address changes. Bound lets suppliers verify their wallet, and lets your system block payments to unverified or changed payees. I'm looking for one design partner to test this on real supplier invoices. Could we speak for 15 minutes this week? Oluwaseun, founder of Bound

Segment D, template 1 (wallet)
Hi, address poisoning and lookalike addresses still drain users. Bound gives wallets a single call that says whether a recipient address belongs to a verified business (domain plus legal name). It's a small integration and I can send a pull request or SDK snippet. Could we do 15 minutes to see if it fits your send screen? Oluwaseun, founder of Bound

Segment D, template 2 (exchange/ramp)
Hi, for business withdrawals, a check against a verified-supplier registry can catch changed or spoofed addresses before funds leave. Bound offers this by API, priced per check. I'd like 15 minutes to walk through it and learn what a compliance team would need to trial it. Open to a letter of intent for a limited pilot. Oluwaseun, founder of Bound

Segment E, template 1 (association/chamber)
Hello, I'm building a free tool that lets Nigerian businesses prove their wallet belongs to them, so overseas buyers paying in stablecoins can't be redirected to a fraudster. I'd like to give a short walkthrough to your members who export or receive stablecoin payments. Could we have a 15-minute call to see if that fits an upcoming session? Oluwaseun, founder of Bound

Segment E, template 2 (event/community organizer)
Hi, I'm a founder based in Abuja working on supplier wallet verification for stablecoin payments. Suppliers verify once, free, and buyers can check them. Could I take 10 minutes at your October meetup to help five businesses verify on mainnet live? I can share the steps beforehand. Oluwaseun, founder of Bound
